## Decisions
- Preserve existing native target-specific compiler and shared buildArtifactPipeline; no broad catch-all retry, raw upstream fallback, DIRECT workaround, new converter or duplicate resolver.
- Treat partial empty policy groups as a recoverable selection/configuration failure for the already established primary-only fallback path. Normal DIRECT/REJECT rules must not prohibit recovery, but a valid builtin policy must remain unchanged. Structural resolver errors, risk, node capabilities and chains remain blockers. This deliberately supersedes old plan scenario5's blanket partial-group fail-closed rule; historical delivery is not rewritten.
- Parsed entries are snapshots, not the node inventory or the user-intent ledger. Keep two useful snapshots plus explicit/reference exceptions, not every fetch's full parse. Keep compact recent fetch summaries. Preserve source evidence and connection provenance rather than breaking refs to force a quota.
- Durable classification overrides are required before pruning: absent overrides otherwise disappear with old entries, and cleared intent could resurrect from surviving historical rows. One ledger is authoritative; no old-entry fallback path.
- Runtime deletion frees reusable pages, not necessarily OS bytes. Existing verified-backup/offline maintenance + separately authorized VACUUM is the physical reclamation path; do not automatically VACUUM/startup-migrate a large production backlog. Existing payload-body CLI does not claim to prune parsed entries. Existing source/probe/risk/audit ledgers are outside destructive retention scope pending specific consumer design.
